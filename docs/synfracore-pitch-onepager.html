The problem

Learners today assemble their education from fragments — a DevOps course here, an exam-prep app there, a certification body somewhere else. Progress, identity, and proof-of-skill never connect. Nobody owns the whole learner.

The product

A single account and progress engine, with each subject area ("academy") as a modular plug-in on the same core — same login, same XP system, same certificate wall, same portfolio, across every subject a person ever studies.
