## What is Law & Compliance Awareness?

Every professional should understand their employment contract. Key areas: notice period obligations, non-compete clauses (enforceability in India), statutory benefits (PF, gratuity, ESI), and intellectual property assignment.

## Why This Exists (The Hook)

Most working professionals sign an employment contract, click "I agree" on countless terms of service, and handle personal or company data daily — all without ever having been taught the legal concepts underneath any of it. That gap is exactly where real problems happen: signing away IP rights without realizing it, not knowing a non-compete clause is largely unenforceable in India anyway, or mishandling personal data in a way that violates the DPDP Act without anyone involved intending harm. This page exists to close that specific gap — not to make you a lawyer, but to make you someone who recognizes when a situation actually needs one.

**Analogy** — Think of this page like a basic electrical-safety course for someone who isn't an electrician. You don't need to be a licensed electrician to know not to touch a live wire, to recognize when wiring looks genuinely dangerous, or to know when to call a professional instead of attempting a fix yourself. Practical legal literacy works the same way — you don't need a law degree to recognize an unusually one-sided NDA clause, understand why your notice period matters, or know that "GDPR applies if we might have EU users" is a real compliance question worth asking a lawyer, not guessing at.

**Try it (2 minutes)** — Reason through why non-compete clauses are specifically flagged as having contested "enforceability in India," rather than being treated as automatically binding just because they're signed, without looking anything up: Indian contract law (Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act) generally treats agreements that restrain a person from exercising a lawful profession as void, with narrow exceptions. If an employment contract includes a broad non-compete clause, and Indian courts have historically been skeptical of enforcing post-employment restraints on trade, what should that gap between "what the contract says" and "what a court would actually enforce" tell you about why simply reading a contract's text isn't the same as knowing your actual legal position?

## Learning Modules

### Module 01 — Workplace Rights
*Employment law basics*

Every professional should understand their employment contract. Key areas: notice period obligations, non-compete clauses (enforceability in India), statutory benefits (PF, gratuity, ESI), and intellectual property assignment.

**Topics covered:**

- Employment contract essentials — 🟢 Beginner
- Notice period and termination — 🟡 Intermediate
- PF, ESI and statutory benefits — 🟡 Intermediate

### Module 02 — IT & Data Laws
*PDPB, IT Act, contracts*

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act regulates how personal data is collected and processed. GDPR applies if you handle EU residents' data. Understanding data classification, consent, and breach notification is professional necessity.

**Topics covered:**

- India PDPB (Personal Data Protection) — 🟡 Intermediate
- IT Act 2000 and amendments — 🟡 Intermediate
- GDPR basics for global teams — 🔴 Advanced

### Module 03 — Documentation & Compliance
*Contracts, NDAs, agreements*

Non-disclosure agreements protect confidential information in both directions. SLAs define service obligations and penalties. Open source licenses matter — GPL is copyleft (your code becomes GPL), MIT/Apache are permissive (commercial-friendly).

**Topics covered:**

- NDA key clauses — 🟡 Intermediate
- Service level agreements — 🟡 Intermediate
- Open source license types — 🔴 Advanced
