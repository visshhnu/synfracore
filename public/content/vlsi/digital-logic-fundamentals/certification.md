# Digital Logic & Design Fundamentals — Certification

## Framing note

There is no dominant vendor certification for digital logic design itself — this is foundational academic material, not a tool or platform skill with its own credential. The real, India-context credential is **GATE EC** (Electronics & Communication Engineering paper), where this technology maps directly onto a named, scored section.

## What GATE EC's Digital Circuits Section Actually Tests

1. **Boolean algebra and K-map simplification** — Overview and Fundamentals' entire simplification toolkit.
2. **Combinational circuit design** — adders, multiplexers, decoders (Intermediate's material).
3. **Sequential circuit basics** — flip-flops, latches, and their timing parameters (Advanced's material) — though full finite-state-machine design and datapath composition is this academy's RTL Design & Computer Architecture technology, not tested as its own separate GATE EC section (see that technology's own Certification page for the relevant finding there).
4. **Number systems and codes** — binary, hex, two's complement (Fundamentals' material).
