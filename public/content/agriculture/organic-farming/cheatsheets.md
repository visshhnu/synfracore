# Natural & Organic Farming — Quick Reference

## Input recipes

```bash
# JEEVAMRUT (200 litres — soil microbial inoculant)
10 kg desi cow dung + 5L desi cow urine
2 kg jaggery + 2 kg gram flour
Handful of soil from under a large tree
Ferment 48 hours, stir twice daily
Apply: 10% dilution, drench soil every 15 days

# COMPOST (ready in 45-60 days)
Ratio: 3 parts dry (leaves, cardboard) : 1 part wet (kitchen waste)
Add cow dung as activator
Turn every 7-10 days, keep moist not waterlogged
Apply: 1-2 kg per sq. metre before planting

# NEEM SPRAY (broad-spectrum organic pesticide)
5 ml neem oil + 1-2 ml liquid soap per litre water
Spray both sides of leaves, in the evening
Repeat every 7-10 days preventively
Targets: aphids, whitefly, mites, caterpillars, fungal disease

# CHILLI-GARLIC SPRAY (chewing pests)
10 green chillies + 10 garlic cloves, blended in 1L water
Strain, dilute 1:10, spray on affected plants

# BORDEAUX MIXTURE (fungal disease — apply BEFORE monsoon)
100g copper sulphate + 100g lime in 10L water
Apply preventively to avoid blight and rust
```

## Container depth by crop (terrace/home garden)

| Depth | Crops |
|---|---|
| Shallow (6–8 in) | Coriander, methi, spinach, radish, microgreens |
| Medium (10–12 in) | Tomato, chilli, capsicum, beans, cucumber, okra |
| Deep (14–18 in) | Carrot, potato, beetroot, turmeric, ginger |

**Container soil mix:** 40% garden soil + 30% vermicompost + 20% cocopeat + 10% sand

## Seasonal planting (most of India)

| Season | Months | Crops |
|---|---|---|
| Summer | Feb–Jun | Okra, cucumber, bitter gourd, amaranth |
| Monsoon | Jul–Oct | Leafy greens, ginger, turmeric, beans |
| Winter (best) | Oct–Feb | Tomato, chilli, peas, carrot, cauliflower |

Coastal zones: expect elevated fungal pressure year-round, not just monsoon. Hill regions: shift winter crops earlier. Semi-arid: mulch + drip irrigation are required, not optional.

## Certification quick reference

| | PGS-India | NPOP |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Paid, third-party audited |
| Model | Farmer peer-review group (5+ members) | External auditor |
| Conversion period | ~2 years, documented | Set by auditing body |
| Use case | Domestic sale, small/home growers | Export, retailers requiring 3rd-party cert |

## Water conservation quick numbers

| Method | Saving | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Drip irrigation | ~60% water | DIY kit for 500 sq.ft: ₹3,000–5,000 |
| Mulching | ~50% water | 3–4 inches dry leaves/rice straw |
| Farm pond (1 acre) | Season buffer | 20×20×2m lined pond, ₹25,000–50,000, often subsidised |

## ZBNF's four pillars (quick list)

1. **Beejamrut** — seed treatment, applied before planting
2. **Jeevamrut** — soil microbial inoculant, applied every 15 days
3. **Mulching** — water conservation + continuous in-place composting
4. **Waaphasa** — soil aeration/moisture management to prevent waterlogged roots
