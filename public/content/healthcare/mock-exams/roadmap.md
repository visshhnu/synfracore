# Mock Exams & CPC Prep — Learning Roadmap

## Estimated Time to Job-Ready
**4-6 weeks** of focused practice-exam work, layered on top of completed CPT/ICD-10-CM/HCPCS/coding-guidelines study — this track is about converting existing knowledge into exam performance, not learning new coding content from scratch.

## Phase 1: Baseline Assessment (Week 1)

- Take one full-length timed practice exam under realistic conditions to establish a baseline score and category-level accuracy
- Complete Project 1 (timed practice-exam log) from this course's Projects section

**Checkpoint:** do you know, with a number attached, your current accuracy in each major category (E/M, surgical coding, modifiers, ICD-10-CM guidelines, HCPCS)? If not, that's the first gap to close.

## Phase 2: Targeted Weak-Area Drilling (Week 2-3)

- Convert every missed practice question into a linked drill: correct answer, your error, and the specific rule involved
- Focus additional review time on your 2-3 weakest categories from Phase 1, not on categories you're already strong in
- Complete Project 2 (weak-area drill deck) from this course's Projects section

**Checkpoint:** has your accuracy in your weakest starting category measurably improved on a fresh set of practice questions?

## Phase 3: Full-Length Timed Practice (Week 3-5)

- Run additional full-length timed mock exams spaced at least a week apart, simulating real exam conditions as closely as possible
- Track score and category-accuracy trends across all exams taken so far
- Complete Project 3 (full mock-exam cycle with trend analysis) from this course's Projects section

**Checkpoint:** is your score trending upward across successive mock exams, or plateaued? A plateau after several attempts usually means it's time to revisit fundamentals in a specific category rather than take more mocks.

## Phase 4: Exam-Day Readiness (Week 5-6)

- Confirm current exam logistics: format, time limit, open-book policy, and passing score, verified against current official guidance rather than assumed from older prep material
- Do a final full-length timed run under conditions matching exam day as closely as possible
- Review this course's Interview Q&A and Certification Guide material one final time

## Common Pitfalls Specific to Mock-Exam Prep

- **Practicing untimed or with unrestricted references** — if the real exam has time pressure and specific reference rules, practicing without those constraints doesn't build the skill that actually gets tested
- **Re-taking the same practice exam repeatedly** — this measures memorization of that specific exam, not real coding proficiency; use varied question sets
- **Ignoring category-level accuracy in favor of the overall score** — a passing overall score can hide one badly weak category that a real exam could easily expose more heavily
- **Studying from outdated practice material** — exam content is periodically revised to track current guidelines; confirm your practice material reflects the current exam version before relying on it

## Getting Exam-Ready and Job-Ready

1. **Portfolio:** the 3 projects in this course's Projects section, showing a documented score trend, not just a single pass/fail result
2. **Certification:** confirm your target credential (CPC, CCA, CCS, BCHHC, or other) and its current exam format directly from AAPC/AHIMA/the issuing body before your final prep phase — see this course's own Certification Guide as a starting point
3. **Resume:** once certified, list the credential and the date earned — for pre-certification roles, a documented, improving mock-exam trend is a reasonable substitute signal
4. **Community:** AAPC/AHIMA study groups and exam-specific forums are useful for calibrating whether your practice difficulty matches the real exam
5. **Interview prep:** be ready to explain your study process, not just your target score — employers value the discipline as much as the credential itself
