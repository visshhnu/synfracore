# General & Banking Awareness — Key Takeaways

- **This topic is unusually time-sensitive — treat every specific rate, name, and scheme parameter as needing current verification**, not something safe to memorize once from any single source, including this page. Structural/definitional knowledge (what repo rate means, how CRR differs from SLR) is stable; specific current values are not.
- **Repo rate (RBI lends to banks) and reverse repo rate (RBI borrows from banks) are inverse mechanisms** — a frequently and precisely tested distinction.
- **CRR is cash held with RBI (earning nothing); SLR is held by the bank itself in approved securities** — the specific, commonly-confused distinction worth getting exactly right.
- **The MPC's 6-member structure and regular meeting cadence is set by statute (the 2016 RBI Act amendment)** — more stable than specific rate decisions, but current membership still needs verification.
- **RTGS/NEFT/IMPS differ structurally** (real-time individual settlement vs. batched settlement vs. instant 24x7 for smaller amounts) — know the structural distinction; exact current limits and operating hours need fresh verification.
- **Scheme questions test objectives and eligibility structure, not just names** — knowing what problem a scheme was designed to solve outperforms memorizing scheme names alone on the actual question style these exams use.
- **CPI, not WPI, is the index RBI's monetary policy is formally targeted against** — a specific, frequently-tested distinction between the two inflation indices.
- **Historical facts (bank nationalization, RBI's founding/nationalization) are stable and safe to memorize confidently** — unlike current rates and officeholders, this content doesn't go stale.
- **SARFAESI enables direct collateral enforcement; IBC is a broader, multi-creditor insolvency resolution process** — different mechanisms for different scenarios, a commonly-tested distinction at advanced level.
- **Digital banking/fintech regulation is an especially fast-moving sub-area** — treat any specific claim here as needing more urgent, fresh verification than most other content on this page.
