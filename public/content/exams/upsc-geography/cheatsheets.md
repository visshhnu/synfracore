## 2. Key theories and models, by geographer

| Geographer / Thinker | Model / Theory | One-line core idea |
|---|---|---|
| Alfred Wegener | Continental Drift | Continents were once joined (Pangaea) and have since drifted apart |
| Thomas Malthus | Malthusian theory | Population grows geometrically, food supply arithmetically — leading to checks |
| Karl Marx (Marxian view) | Marxian population theory | Population pressure is a product of capitalist resource distribution, not a natural law |
| W.S. Thompson / Demographic Transition | Demographic Transition Model | Population growth passes through stages linked to a society's development level |
| Walter Christaller | Central Place Theory | Settlements are hierarchically arranged hexagonal market areas around service centers |
| August Losch | Central Place Theory (modified) | Refines Christaller's model allowing for irregular, profit-maximising market areas |
| Francois Perroux / Jacques Boudeville | Growth Pole Theory | Economic growth concentrates at "poles" and spreads outward (or fails to) |
| Johann Heinrich von Thunen | Agricultural Location Model | Land use around a market forms concentric rings based on transport cost vs. land rent |
| Alfred Weber | Industrial Location Theory | Industry locates to minimize total transport and labour cost (least-cost location) |
| W.W. Rostow | Stages of Economic Growth | Economies progress through five linear stages from traditional to high mass consumption |
| Halford Mackinder | Heartland Theory | Whoever controls the Eurasian "Heartland" controls the World-Island and, potentially, the world |
| Nicholas Spykman | Rimland Theory | The coastal "Rimland" (not the Heartland) is the key to global power |
| Wladimir Koppen | Koppen Climate Classification | Climate types classified by temperature/precipitation thresholds tied to vegetation |
| C.W. Thornthwaite | Thornthwaite Classification | Climate classified using a moisture index based on precipitation effectiveness |
| Glenn Trewartha | Trewartha Classification | A modified, more regionally refined version of Koppen's system |

## 3. Common map/terminology quick-glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Isostasy | The equilibrium between the Earth's crust and mantle — crust "floats" and adjusts to loading/unloading |
| Geosyncline | A large, trough-like depression where thick sediment accumulates, later folded into mountains |
| Denudation chronology | The sequence of erosional events shaping a landscape's history |
| Erosion surface | A relatively flat land surface produced by prolonged erosion, later possibly uplifted |
| Frontogenesis | The formation of a front where two air masses of different properties meet |
| Insolation | Incoming solar radiation received at the Earth's surface |
| Primate city | A country's largest city, disproportionately larger than the next-ranked city, dominating national life |
| Rank-size rule | An empirical pattern where a city's population is inversely proportional to its rank |
| Conurbation | A large urban area formed by the merging of separate cities/towns |
| Agro-climatic zone | A land unit classified by climate and soil suitability for specific crops |
| Command area | The area that can be irrigated by a given irrigation project |
| Western disturbance | An extratropical storm from the Mediterranean bringing winter rain/snow to north India |
| EIA | Environmental Impact Assessment — a study of a project's likely environmental effects before approval |
| SEZ | Special Economic Zone — an area with distinct economic laws to boost trade/investment |

## 4. Revision strategy

**2-3 months before exam:**
- Finish first read-through of both papers; complete Unit 5 (Paper I) models one-pagers and 6-8 state case studies (Paper II).

**1 month before:**
- Solve the last 10 years of PYQs for both papers under timed conditions.
- Redraw the six map-drill categories from memory weekly.

**1 week before:**
- Only previous year papers and your own condensed notes — no new sources.
- Time at least one full mock paper per section.

**1 day before:**
- Light revision only: your models one-pagers, state case studies, and core outline map — no new topics.
