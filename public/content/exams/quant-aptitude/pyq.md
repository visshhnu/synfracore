# Quantitative Aptitude — Previous Year Pattern Questions

Representative practice questions in the style and difficulty of real banking/SSC/railway exam quant sections — not verbatim reproductions of a specific year's paper, but the same patterns those exams repeatedly test.

**Q1.** A sum of ₹12,000 is lent at 10% simple interest per annum. In how many years will it become ₹18,000?
(a) 4 years    (b) 4.5 years    **(c) 5 years**    (d) 6 years

Interest needed = 18000 − 12000 = 6000. Using SI = PRT/100: 6000 = (12000 × 10 × T)/100 → T = 5 years.

---

**Q2.** The ratio of ages of A and B is 3:4. After 6 years, the ratio becomes 4:5. What is A's current age?
(a) 12    **(b) 18**    (c) 15    (d) 20

Let ages be 3x, 4x. (3x+6)/(4x+6) = 4/5 → 5(3x+6) = 4(4x+6) → 15x+30 = 16x+24 → x = 6. A's age = 3×6 = **18**.

---

**Q3.** A boat's speed in still water is 12 km/h and the stream's speed is 4 km/h. Find the time taken to travel 32 km downstream.
(a) 1.5 hours    **(b) 2 hours**    (c) 2.5 hours    (d) 3 hours

Downstream speed = 12 + 4 = 16 km/h. Time = 32/16 = **2 hours**.

---

**Q4.** A can complete a work in 18 days, B in 24 days. They work together for 6 days, then A leaves. In how many more days will B finish the remaining work?
(a) 8 days    (b) 9 days    **(c) 10 days**    (d) 12 days

Combined rate = 1/18 + 1/24 = 4/72 + 3/72 = 7/72 per day. Work done in 6 days = 42/72 = 7/12. Remaining = 5/12. B's rate = 1/24, so time = (5/12) ÷ (1/24) = (5/12) × 24 = **10 days**.

---

**Q5.** A 240 m long train crosses a platform of length 160 m in 20 seconds. What is the train's speed in km/h?
(a) 60 km/h    (b) 66 km/h    **(c) 72 km/h**    (d) 78 km/h

Total distance = 240 + 160 = 400 m. Speed = 400/20 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = **72 km/h**.

---

**Q6.** Data Interpretation: A pie chart shows a company's expenses — Salaries 40%, Rent 20%, Marketing 15%, Utilities 10%, Other 15%. If total expenses are ₹50 lakh, what is the combined amount spent on Rent and Marketing?
(a) ₹15 lakh    (b) ₹16 lakh    **(c) ₹17.5 lakh**    (d) ₹20 lakh

Rent + Marketing = 20% + 15% = 35% of 50 lakh = **₹17.5 lakh**.

## A note on exam-specific numeric patterns

Exact section-wise question counts, time allocations, and marking schemes vary by exam (SBI vs. IBPS vs. SSC vs. railway) and change between exam cycles. > **Note (unverified numbers):** if you're relying on a specific current exam's exact pattern (question count, time limit, negative marking fraction) for your actual preparation strategy, verify it against that exam's current official notification rather than any general figure — these details are exactly the kind of thing that shifts from year to year and shouldn't be assumed stable.
