# Renewable Energy Business — Fundamentals

Overview introduced the four renewable-energy business models and flagged PM-KUSUM's live phase transition. This page covers the real steps to start — starting with the one step that gates almost all scheme-linked installation work.

## Step 1 — State Nodal Agency (SNA) empanelment (for any scheme-linked work)

If your business model involves PM-KUSUM or any other subsidy-linked installation work, empanelment with your state's Renewable Energy nodal agency is the actual gating step — not optional, and not something a strong sales pitch can substitute for:

```
The real process, two stages:
  1. Empanel with your STATE's nodal agency (not a central body --
     each state runs its own list). Real examples: GEDA (Gujarat),
     MEDA (Maharashtra), RREC (Rajasthan), KREDL (Karnataka), TEDA
     (Tamil Nadu).
  2. Bid in competitive tenders run by that same agency -- the lowest
     evaluated bid (L1) typically wins the tender, meaning empanelment
     gets you INTO the process, not an automatic contract.

A mandatory Annual Maintenance Contract (commonly 5 years) is usually
bundled into the installation contract under PM-KUSUM specifically --
meaning the business relationship with a client extends well past the
initial installation, which matters directly for revenue planning
(see Advanced).
```

**(needs verification — recheck against current source)**: empanelment requirements, documentation, and tender cadence are genuinely state-specific and change — confirm directly with your state's nodal agency before assuming a process described for one state applies to another.

## Step 2 — Core business registration (applies to every track)

- **Company structure**: Private Limited, LLP, or Partnership via MCA, same as any registered Indian business.
- **Udyam (MSME) registration**: free, online, and the gateway to most of the financing in Step 4.
- **Electrical contractor license**: required for the actual installation work in most states — a separate credential from business registration itself, typically requiring a licensed electrical supervisor on staff or as a partner.
- **GST registration**: mandatory above the relevant turnover threshold, and needed in practice for tender participation and B2B installation contracts regardless of turnover.

## Step 3 — Track-specific considerations

| Track | Key additional requirement |
|---|---|
| PM-KUSUM Empanelled Vendor | State nodal agency empanelment (Step 1) — the dominant gating factor |
| Rooftop Solar Installer | Often requires DISCOM (electricity distribution company) net-metering approval processes on behalf of clients — a real operational skill, not just a technical installation one |
| Solar EPC (larger commercial/industrial) | May require additional structural/civil engineering sign-off depending on project scale and site |
| O&M Provider | Lightest new-registration burden of the four, but success depends on service-quality reputation built through the other three tracks |

## Try It (2 minutes)

Before assuming PM-KUSUM empanelment guarantees revenue: if your state's next tender cycle is 6 months away and your business has no other revenue source in the meantime, does the business survive that gap? A business plan built entirely around a single scheme's tender cycle, with no other revenue track (rooftop sales, O&M contracts on existing installations) to bridge the gap, is fragile in exactly the way this academy's examples keep illustrating.
