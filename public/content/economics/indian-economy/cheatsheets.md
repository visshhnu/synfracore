# Indian Economy — Cheatsheets

## Planning eras

| Era | Approach | Key feature |
|---|---|---|
| 1951–2014 | Five Year Plans | Centralized allocation via Planning Commission |
| 2015–present | NITI Aayog | Advisory, cooperative-federalism model, no direct fund allocation |

## 1991 reforms — LPG

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Liberalization | Dismantling License Raj — reducing licensing/permit requirements |
| Privatization | Reducing state ownership/control, expanding private-enterprise role |
| Globalization | Opening economy to foreign trade and investment (FDI reforms) |

Trigger: 1991 balance-of-payments crisis (critically low forex reserves).

## Sectoral composition — key pattern

|---|---|
| Agriculture | Declining GDP share; still employs disproportionately large workforce share |
| Industry | Moderate GDP share |
| Services | Now largest GDP contributor; hasn't absorbed labor at same scale as industry-led growth elsewhere |

## Poverty and inequality measurement

| Measure | What it captures |
|---|---|
| Poverty line | Minimum consumption expenditure threshold (rural/urban, periodically revised — Tendulkar, Rangarajan committees) |
| Multidimensional poverty | Deprivation across health, education, living standards (not just income) |
| Gini coefficient | Income/consumption inequality, 0 (equal) to 1 (unequal) |
| Income-share analysis | Share of national income by population decile |

## Union Budget structure

| Component | Includes |
|---|---|
| Revenue Budget | Revenue receipts (taxes) + revenue expenditure (salaries, subsidies, interest) |
| Capital Budget | Capital receipts (borrowing, disinvestment) + capital expenditure (infrastructure) |
| Fiscal deficit | Total expenditure minus total receipts (excluding borrowing) |

Quality distinction: capital-expenditure-funded deficit generally viewed more favorably than revenue-expenditure-funded deficit.
