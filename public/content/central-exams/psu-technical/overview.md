# PSU Technical Exams — Overview

## What this technology covers

PSU Technical Exams covers recruitment preparation for India's major Public Sector Undertakings — BHEL, NTPC, ONGC, BEL, and GAIL among others — spanning the two dominant recruitment routes (GATE-score-based recruitment and PSU-specific standalone technical exams), the typical three-stage selection process (technical/aptitude written exam, group discussion where applicable, and personal/HR interview), and the engineering-discipline-specific technical content each PSU actually tests. This technology treats PSU recruitment as a distinct preparation track from GATE itself (a separate exam prep concern) or from general government-exam preparation, since PSU selection combines technical depth with a genuinely different aptitude and interview format than either.

## Why it matters

PSUs remain among the most sought-after employers for engineering graduates in India — offering government-job-level job security combined with technical/engineering career tracks, competitive compensation, and (for many PSUs) direct GATE-score-based recruitment that doesn't require a separate written exam at all. Understanding which PSUs use which recruitment route (GATE-score vs. standalone exam), and preparing for the specific format each uses, is a genuinely different preparation problem than generic "engineering exam prep" — a candidate targeting NTPC via GATE score needs a different preparation strategy than one targeting a PSU using its own standalone technical exam.

## Two recruitment routes — a quick map

| Route | How it works | Example PSUs |
|---|---|---|
| GATE-score-based | PSU directly uses a candidate's GATE score for shortlisting, no separate written technical exam | NTPC, GAIL, BHEL (for many disciplines/years) |
| Standalone PSU exam | PSU conducts its own technical + aptitude written exam independent of GATE | ONGC, BEL (for many disciplines/years), and PSUs not participating in GATE-based recruitment for a given cycle |

## The three-stage selection process

Most PSU recruitment (regardless of route) follows a broadly similar downstream process after initial shortlisting: a **technical + aptitude written exam** (for standalone-route PSUs) or direct shortlisting by GATE score (for GATE-route PSUs), often followed by a **group discussion** (used by some PSUs, not universal), and finally a **personal/HR interview** assessing both technical depth and role/organizational fit. The technical and HR stages are weighted differently across PSUs — some weight the interview heavily even after strong written/GATE performance, making interview preparation a genuinely high-stakes, not optional, part of PSU preparation.

## Exam and career relevance

PSU Technical Exams preparation is directly relevant to any engineering graduate (primarily Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Civil, and Chemical disciplines, varying by PSU) targeting a public-sector engineering career — a distinct and substantial career track alongside private-sector engineering roles, state PSC technical postings, and further postgraduate study.
