# PSU Technical Exams — Cheatsheets

## Recruitment route comparison

| Property | GATE-route | Standalone-exam route |
|---|---|---|
| Technical exam | GATE score used directly | PSU-specific written exam |
| Example PSUs | NTPC, GAIL, BHEL (varies by year) | ONGC, BEL (varies by year) |
| Preparation focus | Strong GATE preparation | GATE-level technical + PSU-specific aptitude/pattern review |
| Standardization | High (GATE is uniform) | Low (varies PSU to PSU) |

`(needs verification — recheck against current source: recruitment route can change year to year per PSU.)`

## Typical selection stages

| Stage | Assesses | Universal? |
|---|---|---|
| Technical + aptitude written exam / GATE score | Core discipline knowledge, quantitative/logical reasoning | Yes (one or the other) |
| Group discussion | Communication, collaboration, leadership | No — PSU/cycle dependent |
| Personal/HR interview | Applied judgment, depth of understanding, organizational fit | Yes |

## Discipline-to-PSU alignment (general pattern)

| Discipline | Especially relevant PSUs | Core technical emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical | NTPC, BHEL | Thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, machine design |
| Electrical | NTPC, BHEL | Power systems, electrical machines, control systems |
| Electronics & Communication | BEL | Communication systems, signal processing, electronic devices |
| Chemical | ONGC, GAIL | Process engineering, thermodynamics, refining/gas processing |

## Exam component breakdown (standalone-route PSUs, typical)

| Component | Tests |
|---|---|
| Technical | Discipline-specific core engineering knowledge |
| Aptitude | Quantitative reasoning, logical reasoning, verbal ability |
| General awareness | Often sector-specific (energy policy, PSU's own recent developments) |

## Interview preparation focus areas

| Area | What's assessed |
|---|---|
| Technical depth | Genuine understanding vs. memorized answers (via "why"/"how" follow-ups) |
| Applied judgment | Scenario-based engineering problem-solving |
| PSU/sector knowledge | Understanding of the specific PSU's business and current challenges |
| Organizational fit | Career motivation, "why this PSU" |
