# CA Final — Quick Reference

## Paper structure

| Group | Paper | Subject | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | 1 | Financial Reporting | Ind AS, consolidated FS, EPS, complex standards |
| I | 2 | Advanced Financial Management | Capital budgeting, portfolio theory, derivatives, forex, M&A valuation |
| I | 3 | Advanced Auditing & Professional Ethics | SAs, misconduct, complex entity audits |
| II | 4 | Direct Tax Laws & Int'l Taxation | Advanced income tax, transfer pricing, DTAA |
| II | 5 | Indirect Tax Laws | GST (advanced), customs — **only GST-heavy paper** |
| II | 6 | Integrated Business Solutions | Multi-disciplinary case study — strategy+finance+law+tax |

## Key misconception to avoid

```
"CA Final = advanced tax" — WRONG
Group I (Financial Reporting, AFM) is typically HARDER for
most candidates than tax. Allocate prep time accordingly.
```

## Application-over-memorization shift

```
Intermediate: apply ONE standard to a clean scenario
CA Final: apply MULTIPLE interacting standards/techniques to
  a complicated scenario with embedded, non-obvious issues
Practice: complex integrated problems, not isolated topic review
```

## DTAA analysis method

```
1. Characterize the income under BOTH domestic law and the DTAA
2. Compare domestic withholding rate vs. DTAA rate (lower generally applies)
3. Confirm eligibility conditions (TRC, beneficial ownership tests)
```

## Paper 6 — distinct preparation needed

```
Papers 1-5 mastery does NOT automatically transfer to Paper 6
Needs DEDICATED case-study practice — the tested skill is
INTEGRATION (identifying which facts matter to which
sub-question, how one area's conclusion constrains another)
```

## Final-month priority framework

```
1. Genuinely weak areas, ranked by MARKS IMPACT (not just discomfort)
2. Confirmed high-weightage topics (actual syllabus, not assumption)
3. Recently-updated content (current Finance Act, Ind AS amendments)
   — verify against the specific exam session's official material
```

## Group sequencing considerations

```
Group I first: builds on fresh Intermediate accounting/finance
  foundation; tackles the harder group while motivation is high
Group II first: fits tax-heavy articleship exposure; more
  procedural/rule-based content to build confidence first
No universal answer — decide based on YOUR articleship exposure
```

## AICITSS

```
Must be completed before sitting CA Final — typically during
final year of articleship. Complete early, don't defer.
```

## Cross-subject question recognition

```
Real transactions often have simultaneous implications across
papers (e.g., a lease: Ind AS treatment AND GST implications
in ONE question) — practice recognizing and handling this,
not just deepening each subject in isolation.
```
