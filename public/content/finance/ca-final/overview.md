# CA Final — Group I & Group II

## Why This Is Not "Just GST"

CA Final is the last and hardest stage of the ICAI qualification — and it is much broader than any single subject. Collapsing it to a GST/taxation page badly undersells what's actually tested. Group II alone has four papers beyond tax, and Group I is dominated by financial reporting and strategic finance, not tax at all.

## Group I

| Paper | Subject | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Financial Reporting | Ind AS, consolidated financial statements, EPS, complex accounting standards |
| 2 | Advanced Financial Management | Capital budgeting, portfolio theory, derivatives, forex, M&A valuation |
| 3 | Advanced Auditing & Professional Ethics | Standards on Auditing, professional misconduct, audit of complex entities |

## Group II

| Paper | Subject | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Direct Tax Laws & International Taxation | Advanced income tax, transfer pricing, DTAA |
| 5 | Indirect Tax Laws | GST (advanced), customs law — **this is the only paper that's GST-heavy** |
| 6 | Integrated Business Solutions (Multi-disciplinary case study) | Combines strategy, finance, law, and tax in one case-based paper |

## AICITSS Requirement

Before sitting for CA Final, candidates must complete **AICITSS** (Advanced Integrated Course on Information Technology and Soft Skills) — usually during the final year of articleship.

## How Candidates Typically Sequence It

Most candidates attempt Group I and Group II in separate sittings rather than together, given the sheer volume — clearing one group while continuing to work reduces the all-or-nothing pressure of attempting both simultaneously.

## What Makes CA Final Different From Intermediate

- **Application over memorisation**: questions are scenario-based, often requiring you to combine two subjects (e.g., a tax implication inside a financial reporting question).
- **Case-study paper (Paper 6)**: genuinely multi-disciplinary — tests judgment across strategy, law, tax, and finance in one integrated scenario, not a single subject in isolation.
- **Pass rates are lower** than Intermediate — reflecting the qualification's final gatekeeping role before you can call yourself a Chartered Accountant.

:::warning Common misconception
"CA Final = advanced tax" is the single most common misunderstanding about this stage. Financial Reporting and Advanced Financial Management (Group I) are typically the hardest papers for most candidates — not tax.
:::
