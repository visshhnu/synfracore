# Financial Markets — Cheatsheets

## Equity vs. debt comparison

| Property | Equity | Debt |
|---|---|---|
| Represents | Ownership stake | Loan to issuer |
| Return | Dividends + price appreciation (variable) | Fixed coupon interest (predictable) |
| Risk | Higher | Lower |
| Liquidation priority | Paid after debt holders | Paid before equity holders |

## Bond terminology

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Coupon | Periodic interest payment |
| Face value | Principal repaid at maturity |
| Maturity date | When principal is repaid |
| Yield | Effective return, moves inversely to price |

## Mutual fund types

| Type | Management style | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Actively managed | Fund manager makes discretionary choices | Outperform benchmark |
| Passive/Index fund | Replicates a specific index | Match benchmark performance |

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NAV (Net Asset Value) | Per-unit value of fund holdings, calculated once daily |
| Unit | An investor's proportional holding in the fund |

## Derivatives quick reference

| Instrument | Obligation? | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Futures | Yes — both parties obligated | Standardized contract, predetermined price/date |
| Options | No — buyer has right, not obligation | Premium paid for the right to transact |

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hedging | Using a derivative to reduce existing risk exposure |
| Speculation | Using a derivative to take on new risk exposure for profit |
| Leverage | Controlling a large position with a smaller capital commitment — amplifies gains and losses |
| Strike price | Predetermined transaction price in an options contract |
