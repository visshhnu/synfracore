# CA Intermediate — Complete Guide

**Before you start:** [CA/CS/CMA Foundation](/academies/finance/ca-cs-foundation/overview) (or the Direct Entry route for Commerce graduates) is assumed as the entry point into this level.

## Why This Exists (The Hook)

Foundation tests whether you can handle the basic building blocks — simple accounting, elementary business law, basic quantitative aptitude. Intermediate is where those building blocks combine into the actual professional skill: 8 papers spanning advanced accounting standards, corporate law, taxation, and cost management, deliberately structured across two groups so you can clear one at a time rather than needing to master everything simultaneously. This is also where articleship begins — Intermediate is the gate that unlocks the practical training the CA qualification is built around.

**Analogy** — Think of CA Intermediate like the core coursework in a professional degree, sitting between general prerequisites and a specialized thesis. Foundation is like general-education prerequisites — broad, foundational, not yet specialized. Intermediate is like the core major coursework — genuinely deep, subject-specific material (advanced accounting, corporate law, taxation) that a professional in the field actually needs, still building toward but not yet at the highest specialization level. CA Final, which comes after, is like the thesis stage — applying everything learned to complex, integrated real-world scenarios.

**Try it (2 minutes)** — Reason through why groups can be cleared separately rather than requiring all 8 papers to be passed simultaneously, without looking anything up: each group has its own 50% aggregate + 40%-per-paper passing requirement, checked independently. If a candidate did well on Group I but narrowly missed Group II's aggregate, what would forcing an all-8-papers-at-once requirement do to that candidate's ability to bank the papers they already passed — and why would letting them retain Group I's clear while re-attempting only Group II reduce the overall exam's difficulty without lowering the actual competency bar for each individual group?

## What is CA Intermediate?

CA Intermediate is the second level of the Chartered Accountancy qualification from ICAI (Institute of Chartered Accountants of India). After clearing Foundation (or through direct entry with Commerce graduates), students appear for Intermediate before the final CA exams.

## CA Intermediate Syllabus (New Course — effective 2023)

### Group I (4 Papers)

**Paper 1: Advanced Accounting (100 marks)**
- Accounting Standards (AS 1–29) — India GAAP
- Ind AS overview (IFRS-converged standards)
- Accounting for partnership firms — goodwill, dissolution, amalgamation
- Branch accounting, consignment accounts
- Hire purchase, lease accounting
- Company accounts — issue/forfeiture of shares, debentures, final accounts
- Consolidated financial statements (basic)
- Insurance claims

**Paper 2: Corporate & Other Laws (100 marks)**
- Companies Act 2013 — Incorporation, Memorandum, Articles, Directors, Meetings, Dividends, Accounts, Audit
- Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code 2016 (basic)
- Prevention of Money Laundering Act 2002 (basic)
- Foreign Exchange Management Act 1999 (FEMA)
- SEBI Regulations (basic — Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements)
- Business Law: Indian Contract Act, Sale of Goods Act, Negotiable Instruments Act, Consumer Protection Act

**Paper 3: Taxation (100 marks)**
- Income Tax:
  - Heads of income — salary, HP, PGBP, CG, OS
  - Deductions under Chapter VIA (80C, 80D, 80G, etc.)
  - Assessment procedure, advance tax, TDS
  - Appeals and rectification
- GST:
  - Supply, levy, exemptions, place of supply
  - Input tax credit, registrations
  - Returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9)
  - Valuation, demand and recovery

**Paper 4: Cost and Management Accounting (100 marks)**
- Cost concepts: material, labour, overhead
- Costing methods: Job, Process, Contract, Batch, Activity-Based
- Marginal costing and CVP analysis
- Standard costing and variance analysis
- Budget and budgetary control
- Management accounting tools: ratio analysis, fund flow, cash flow

### Group II (4 Papers)

**Paper 5: Advanced Accounting (100 marks)**
- Financial instruments (Ind AS 109)
- Revenue recognition (Ind AS 115)
- Leases (Ind AS 116)
- Business combinations (Ind AS 103)
- Consolidated financial statements (advanced)
- Employee benefits (Ind AS 19)

**Paper 6: Auditing & Ethics (100 marks)**
- Nature and scope of auditing
- Standards on Auditing (SA) — SA 200–720
- Audit procedures: planning, risk assessment, evidence
- Internal audit, audit report, CARO 2020
- Company auditor — appointment, qualifications, disqualification
- Professional ethics — Code of Ethics for CAs

**Paper 7: Enterprise Information Systems & Strategic Management (100 marks)**
- EIS: Information systems in business, IT controls, cybersecurity basics, ERP systems
- Strategic Management: SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, BCG matrix, Balanced Scorecard

**Paper 8: Financial Management & Economics for Finance (100 marks)**
- Financial Management: Capital structure, cost of capital, leverage, working capital, dividend policy
- Economic analysis: Demand-supply, market structures, monetary policy, fiscal policy, international trade

---

## Passing Criteria

- Minimum 40% in each paper
- Minimum 50% aggregate in the group (all 4 papers combined)
- Groups can be cleared separately (need not clear both in same attempt)

---

## Articleship (3 Years)

Mandatory practical training under a practising CA:
- Duration: 3 years
- Exposure: Auditing, taxation, accounting, GST returns, company law compliance
- Stipend: ₹2,000–₹15,000/month (varies by city and firm size)
- Big 4 CA firms (Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC) offer ₹12,000–₹20,000/month stipend
- Article transfer allowed in certain circumstances
