# CA Articleship — FAQ

**Do I need to clear both groups of CA Intermediate before starting articleship?**
No — clearing just ONE group of CA Intermediate is sufficient eligibility to begin articleship registration. This is a common misconception that unnecessarily delays some candidates from starting their mandatory 3-year training period earlier than they need to.

**Should I prioritize the highest-stipend offer when choosing a principal/firm?**
Not necessarily — genuine, broad exposure (real audit and tax work, direct client interaction) at a firm offering the ICAI-minimum stipend generally builds more valuable, career-relevant skill than a firm offering a marginally higher stipend for narrow, repetitive work like data entry. The stipend difference is modest and temporary; the skill and exposure difference compounds over the full 3-year period and affects post-qualification career positioning.

**Is it realistic to study for CA Intermediate/Final while working full articleship hours?**
Yes — this is the norm among successful candidates, not an exception. Building a consistent, even if modest, evening/weekend study routine from early in articleship is more sustainable than deferring serious study to a compressed period later, especially since articleship responsibility and workload tend to intensify, not ease, in later years, making an even later start to study genuinely harder to sustain.

**Can I request to be included in client meetings if my firm hasn't offered this exposure by default?**
Yes, and this is a reasonable, common request worth making proactively rather than waiting passively. Firms vary significantly in how much direct client interaction they provide articled clerks by default — asking a supervising senior to include you in an upcoming client meeting, even as an observer initially, is a practical way to build professional communication skills that are difficult to develop through technical study alone.

**What should I do if I'm unhappy with the quality of training at my current firm?**
Consider a direct conversation with your principal about specific concerns first, where realistic — they may not be aware of the issue, and it may resolve without a formal transfer. ICAI does permit articleship transfers under specific conditions, but a transfer resets some continuity requirements and carries real administrative cost, so it's worth treating as a considered decision for genuine, unresolved issues rather than a first response to normal workplace friction.

**When should I complete AICITSS relative to the rest of my articleship?**
As early as possible within the eligible window, rather than deferring it toward the end of articleship. AICITSS must be completed before appearing for CA Final, and it's typically done during the final year of training — but that final year already tends to converge peak articleship responsibility and intensifying CA Final study demands, so completing AICITSS early avoids adding an additional, avoidable time-pressure source to an already demanding period.

**Is industrial training a lesser alternative to a traditional CA firm articleship?**
No — it's a genuine strategic option worth choosing deliberately based on your own career direction, not a fallback. If your career interest leans toward corporate finance, internal audit, or controllership roles rather than public accounting practice, industrial training's internal finance-function exposure is more directly relevant than a traditional firm articleship's external audit/tax focus. The right choice depends on genuine career direction, not a general sense that one path is inherently better than the other.
