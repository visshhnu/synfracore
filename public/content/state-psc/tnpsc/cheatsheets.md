## Group structure quick reference

| Group | Level | Typical stages |
|---|---|---|
| Group I | Senior administrative | Prelims + Mains + Interview |
| Group II | Executive/non-executive | Prelims + Mains (varies) |
| Group III | Junior-level | Prelims + subsequent stage (varies) |
| Group IV | Entry-level | Typically Prelims-only or simplified |

| Stage | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Preliminary | Objective screening exam |
| Mains | Detailed/descriptive exam (senior Groups) |

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|

## Tamil Nadu history — key themes

## Preparation strategy by stage

| Stage | Focus |
|---|---|
| Prelims | Samacheer Kalvi content mastery, objective recall |
| Mains | Dedicated answer-writing practice (distinct skill from recall) |
| Current affairs | Both national and TN-specific developments |

## Common preparation pitfalls

| Pitfall | Correction |
|---|---|
| Assuming NCERT/UPSC prep transfers directly | Study Samacheer Kalvi specifically for TN-weighted content |
| Uniform preparation depth across all Groups | Calibrate depth to targeted Group's actual exam stages |
| Neglecting Mains answer-writing practice | Practice timed, structured answer writing as a distinct skill |
